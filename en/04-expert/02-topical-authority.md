# 02. Topical Authority

**Level:** Expert | **Reading time:** 18 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 02-intermediate/02

## Goal
Build deep, structured topic coverage so the site is known as a source for a specific topic.

## A note on the term
"Topical authority" is an SEO community term, not an official Google metric. Use it as a working framework. For Discover, Google has said authority is assessed per topic and not per domain, which points the same direction as this idea.

## Steps
1. **Map core topics** that align with the business.
2. **List all sub-topics** and questions at each funnel stage.
3. **Check depth:** are there important gaps not yet covered?
4. **Build pillars and supporting pages** with meaningful internal links.
5. **Keep quality consistent.** One cluster with 20 thin pages is worse than 5 strong pages.
6. **Update pillar pages regularly** when information changes.

## Measure
- Number of unique queries in the cluster that produce impressions.
- Position distribution per cluster over time.
- Cluster contribution to key events.

This is GSC and GA4 data, not a made-up authority score.

## Traps
- Chasing all topics at once.
- Writing to close a keyword list without adding value.
- Treating the number of articles as a success indicator.

## Exercise
Pick one cluster. Build a topic map, a gap audit, and a 90-day plan with success measures from GSC data.

## Comprehension check
- Why is topical authority not a Google metric?
- What is the sign that a cluster is too thin?
