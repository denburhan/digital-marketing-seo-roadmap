# 05. Local SEO

**Level:** Intermediate | **Reading time:** 15 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 01-basic/06

## Goal
Make a location-based business easy to find for searches in a specific area.

## Main components
Google names three factors used for local results: **relevance**, **distance**, and **prominence**. You cannot change distance, but you can improve the other two.

## Google Business Profile (GBP)
- Claim and verify the profile.
- Name, address, phone number (NAP) must match your website exactly.
- Choose the most accurate primary category.
- Fill in opening hours, services, service area, and real photos.
- Reply to reviews politely, including negative ones.
- Do not stuff keywords into the business name. That violates GBP guidelines.

## Reviews
Ask real customers for reviews. Do not buy reviews, and do not filter to only the good ones. Fake reviews violate policy and risk removal.

## Local pages on the site
- One service page per city or area only if you truly serve it and have different useful content there. City pages whose content is identical except for the swapped city name fall into the low-value scaled content category.
- Include address, map, opening hours, and how to contact.
- LocalBusiness structured data that matches the page content.

## Citations and local presence
List the business in relevant, trusted directories with consistent NAP. Quality matters more than quantity.

## Measure
Track calls, direction requests, and website clicks from GBP Insights, plus local queries in GSC.

## Exercise
1. Audit the business NAP on the site, GBP, and 3 directories. Note inconsistencies.
2. List 10 local customer questions and answer them on one service page.

## Comprehension check
- What are the three local result factors according to Google?
- When are city-by-city pages considered a problem?
