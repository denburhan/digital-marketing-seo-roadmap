# 08. 30-Day Study Plan (Basic)

**Level:** Basic | **Prerequisite:** modules 01 to 07

Goal: one small site (yours or a practice site) that is verified in GSC, has 5 mapped pages, and is measured.

## Week 1: Foundation
- Day 1-2: read modules 01 and 02. Choose one practice business.
- Day 3-4: modules 03 and 04. Check the practice site's robots.txt and sitemap.
- Day 5-7: set up GSC and GA4.

## Week 2: Research
- Day 8-10: module 05. Collect 50 keywords.
- Day 11-12: group them into clusters and determine intent from the SERP.
- Day 13-14: fill in `templates/keyword-intent-map.csv`.

## Week 3: Production
- Day 15-19: write 2 pages using the module 06 checklist.
- Day 20-21: add internal links and meaningful images.

## Week 4: Measure
- Day 22-25: submit URLs to GSC, monitor index status.
- Day 26-28: measure PageSpeed.
- Day 29-30: write notes: what you learned, what you will repeat.

## Level completion criteria
- [ ] GSC verified, sitemap submitted
- [ ] 1 keyword map file filled in with clear data sources
- [ ] 2 pages pass the on-page checklist
- [ ] Can explain crawl, index, serve without looking at notes

Continue to `02-intermediate/`.
