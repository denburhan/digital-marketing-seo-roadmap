# 01. Entity SEO and Brand

**Level:** Advanced | **Reading time:** 18 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 02-intermediate

## Goal
Help search engines and readers recognize who you are, what you offer, and why you deserve trust.

## What is an entity
An entity is something that can be recognized as a distinct object: a person, organization, place, product, concept. Modern search engines do not only match words, they also understand the relationships between entities.

## Building entity clarity
1. **Clear About and Contact pages.** Who is behind the site, the address, how to reach them.
2. **Name consistency.** Brand name, founder name, address, and description are the same on the site, social profiles, GBP, and directories.
3. **Organization or Person structured data** that matches the facts. No special schema promises ranking. Its function is to help understanding.
4. **Supporting profiles** (LinkedIn, GBP, association profiles) that link to each other and to the official site.
5. **Real authors** with profile pages and a verifiable body of work.

## Beware of misplaced schema
- AggregateRating is valid only on types that really have real reviews (Product, Service, LocalBusiness with genuine user reviews). Attaching it to Organization or without real reviews is risky.
- Check that the schema type fits the niche. Old template leftovers often include unsuitable types.

## Brand as a signal
Brand name searches, organic mentions, and genuine reviews are signs that an entity is known. Do not buy mentions or reviews. Machines and humans can both recognize fake patterns.

## Measure
- Brand searches in GSC (queries containing the brand name).
- Unlinked mentions (monitor manually or through alert tools).
- Knowledge panel or brand profile in search results (check manually, no guarantee).
