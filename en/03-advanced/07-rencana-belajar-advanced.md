# 07. Advanced Study Plan (8 weeks)

**Prerequisite:** pass the 02-intermediate criteria

- **Week 1-2:** entity and brand, E-E-A-T. Result: a consistency table and a trust audit.
- **Week 3-4:** page audit. Result: 3 complete audits with recommendations.
- **Week 5:** index diagnosis. Result: a classification report of 10 URLs.
- **Week 6:** spam policy. Result: a site risk audit.
- **Week 7:** programmatic SEO. Result: a project design.
- **Week 8:** final project.

## Final project
Choose one real site (yours or with the owner's written permission). Produce one document: audit, priorities, execution of 3 actions, measured results from GSC, and a reflection on what worked and what did not.

## Completion criteria
- [ ] Can diagnose why a page is not indexed in order
- [ ] Can reject risky tactics with reasons based on official policy
- [ ] Produces an audit with one firm recommendation backed by numbers

Continue to `04-expert/`.
