# 01. What Is Digital Marketing

**Level:** Basic | **Reading time:** 10 minutes | **Prerequisite:** none

## Goal
After this module you can explain digital marketing in your own words, name the main channels, and say why SEO is a foundation.

## Short definition
Digital marketing is every effort to introduce, sell, and retain customers through digital channels: search engines, social media, email, marketplaces, paid ads, and your own website.

The difference from traditional marketing: almost every interaction can be measured. You can know how many people saw, clicked, and bought. But "measurable" does not mean "automatically correct". Numbers that are set up wrong still mislead, which is why the analytics module in the next level matters.

## Three types of media
- **Owned media:** assets you control. Website, blog, email list, app.
- **Earned media:** attention you get without paying directly. Reviews, press coverage, mentions, natural backlinks, recommendations.
- **Paid media:** space you buy. Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, paid endorsements.

Rule of thumb: build owned media first. Paid media stops working the moment the budget stops. Owned media keeps working.

## Main channels
- **SEO (Search Engine Optimization):** improving a website so it appears in organic search results.
- **SEM / search ads:** paying to appear in search results.
- **Content marketing:** articles, videos, and guides that answer what potential buyers need.
- **Social media marketing:** organic and paid on social platforms.
- **Email marketing:** direct communication with a list of contacts who agreed to receive it.
- **Affiliate and partnerships:** other people sell for you on commission.
- **Marketplace marketing:** optimizing your store and listings on marketplaces.

## Why SEO is often the foundation
People who search for something in a search engine already have intent. That is why search traffic is usually "warmer" than traffic that only saw a feed. Also, a page that ranks well keeps working without a cost per click. An honest note: SEO results take time and cannot be promised. Anyone who guarantees "rank 1 in X days" should be treated with suspicion.

## Common beginner mistakes
1. Choosing every channel at once, then working seriously on none.
2. Chasing followers or visitors instead of business results (orders, leads, sign-ups).
3. Having no clear landing page before starting ads or writing.

## Exercise
1. Pick one real business (a neighbor's shop is fine). Write the 3 channels that make the most sense and why.
2. For each channel, write one number you will track.
