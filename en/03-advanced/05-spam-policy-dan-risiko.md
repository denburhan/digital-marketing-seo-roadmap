# 05. Spam Policy and Risk Management

**Level:** Advanced | **Reading time:** 15 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 02-intermediate/06

## Goal
Recognize practices that violate Google's spam policy, so your strategy does not cause a drop or a manual action.

## Violations you need to know
According to the Google Search Central spam policies documentation (checked 20 November 2026), among others:
- **Scaled content abuse:** creating many pages at scale primarily to manipulate ranking and not to help users, whether through automation, humans, or a combination of both.
- **Expired domain abuse:** buying an expired domain and using it to ride on its old reputation with content of almost no value to users.
- **Site reputation abuse:** placing third-party content on a reputable site in order to exploit the main site's signals.
- **Link spam:** buying, selling, or exchanging links that pass ranking signals.
- Classic practices: cloaking, doorway pages, hidden text, keyword stuffing, sneaky redirects.

Read the full list and the official examples in the original documentation. The policy is updated from time to time.

## Automated and AI content
Using AI is not automatically a violation. What is judged is the purpose and the value to users. Mass production of thin pages to catch queries is a prohibited pattern, no matter who or what writes it.

## SpamBrain
Google's spam detection system. It can neutralize manipulative signals without notice. Symptom in the field: activity increases but organic results stagnate.

## Spam reporting
Spam reports to Google can be used for manual actions, and the report text may be passed on to the site owner. Only report with concrete evidence.

## Risk management
- Record all link building and mass content activity with dates.
- Avoid patterns that depend on loopholes. If a tactic only works while Google has not noticed it, that is risk debt.
- Prepare a recovery plan: audit, cleanup, documentation, then a review request if there is a manual action.

## Exercise
Review your site against the violation list above. Write pass or fail findings with evidence.

## Comprehension check
- Is AI-generated content automatically a spam policy violation?
- What do you do after receiving a manual action?
