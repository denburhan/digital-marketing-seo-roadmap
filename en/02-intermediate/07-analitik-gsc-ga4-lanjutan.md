# 07. Advanced Analytics: GSC and GA4

**Level:** Intermediate | **Reading time:** 18 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 01-basic/07

## Goal
Turn data into decisions: what to fix first.

## Analysis patterns in GSC
- **High impressions, low CTR:** improve title and meta description, check whether intent matches.
- **Position 8 to 20 with decent impressions:** candidates for a content and internal link push.
- **Clicks down with no position change:** check SERP changes (new features, AI Overview, ads).
- **Clicks down and position down:** check technical changes, competitors, or algorithm updates.

The threshold numbers above (for example "position 8 to 20") are practical starting points, not official Google numbers.

## Export through the API
The Search Console API lets you export daily data to a spreadsheet or database for long history. Automate it, then analyze trends with code, not screenshots. Principle: measure, do not claim.

## GA4 for SEO
- Segment Organic Search traffic.
- Look at organic landing pages and the key events from those pages.
- Compare behavior per funnel stage.

## Honest attribution
One visitor can come from many channels. Do not claim one channel "produced" all conversions. State the attribution model you use.

## A useful report
One page, three parts: what changed (numbers), why according to the data (hypothesis), what to do next (one firm recommendation with a numeric reason).

## Exercise
1. Build a one-page monthly report from your site's GSC data.
2. Write one firm recommendation and the numbers behind it.

## Comprehension check
- Why is a screenshot not good evidence for a trend?
- What do you check first when clicks drop but position stays the same?
