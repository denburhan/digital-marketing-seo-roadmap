# 04. Strategy and Decision Making

**Level:** Expert | **Reading time:** 20 minutes | **Prerequisite:** all previous modules

## Goal
Use data to decide SEO priorities and communicate choices firmly.

## Principles
1. **Start from the business goal**, not from a list of SEO tasks.
2. **Separate fact, estimate, and opinion.** GSC data and your own analytics are measurement facts. Third-party tool numbers are estimates. The rest is opinion or hypothesis.
3. **One firm recommendation** at the end of every analysis, with a numeric reason. A list of options without a decision makes the reader carry your work.
4. **Label confidence.** "Verified in official documentation", "observation from own data", "author's claim", "hypothesis".
5. **Predictions as estimates, not promises.** Do not promise rankings or percentage increases.

## Building a roadmap
- Technical foundation first if there are crawl and index blockers.
- Then the BOFU pages closest to results.
- Then MOFU and TOFU clusters.
- Review every month with data.

## Reading trends
- Compare equivalent periods (for example 28 days against the previous 28 days, or year over year for seasonality).
- Be wary of a single spike: check whether one query or page is driving it.
- Record external events: algorithm updates, seasons, campaigns.

## Decision report format
1. Situation (numbers).
2. Diagnosis (hypothesis and evidence).
3. Options (at most three, with trade-offs).
4. Firm recommendation and the numeric reason.
5. How to measure the result and when to review.

## Ethics
- Do not touch live ads or production settings destructively without the owner's approval.
- Do not promise results you cannot control.
- Admit mistakes and correct publicly when needed.

## Exercise
Write one complete decision report from the GSC data of a real site, in the five-part format.

## Comprehension check
- Why is a list of options without a recommendation less helpful?
- What should you check when you see a traffic spike?
