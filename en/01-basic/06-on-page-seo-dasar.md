# 06. Basic On-Page SEO

**Level:** Basic | **Reading time:** 15 minutes | **Prerequisite:** module 05

## Goal
Build one page that is clear to humans and easy for machines to understand.

## Elements to work on
- **Title tag.** Describe the page accurately, put the main topic first, avoid repeated words. Google may rewrite the displayed title, and there is no official character limit. Treat length as a display guide, not a rule.
- **Meta description.** A summary for the potential reader. Not a direct ranking factor, but it affects the click decision. Google may also replace it with an excerpt from the page.
- **Headings (H1 to H3).** One H1 that names the topic, H2 for main sections, H3 for details. Headings are a skeleton, not a place to cram keywords.
- **URL.** Short, readable, using words that explain. Pick one form and stay consistent (with or without trailing slash).
- **Short paragraphs.** One idea per paragraph. Core answer at the start of a section.
- **Images.** Descriptive file names, alt text that explains the image content, compressed size.
- **Internal links.** To other relevant pages, with anchors that explain the destination.

## Things you do not need to chase
- Keyword density with a target percentage. It is not a ranking factor.
- "LSI keywords". Google does not use this term. What matters: topic coverage and terms that naturally appear.
- Image EXIF metadata as a ranking factor. Google has never stated this.

## Single-page checklist
- [ ] One purpose, one primary keyword
- [ ] Title and H1 match the content
- [ ] Core answer visible without long scrolling
- [ ] Real evidence: original photos, data, experience
- [ ] At least 2 relevant internal links
- [ ] All images have meaningful alt text
- [ ] Page is comfortable to read on a phone

## Exercise
Take one page of your site. Fill in the checklist above and mark the failures. Fix the three easiest.

## Comprehension check
- Why can the title shown in Google differ from your title tag?
- What is the function of headings besides containing keywords?
