# 04. Basic SEO Terms

**Level:** Basic | **Reading time:** 10 minutes | **Prerequisite:** module 03

## Goal
Master the vocabulary used throughout the material.

## Term list
- **SERP:** search engine results page.
- **Organic:** results that are not paid.
- **Keyword / query:** the word or phrase a user types. A query is what is actually typed, a keyword is what you target.
- **Search intent:** the purpose behind a query. Informational, navigational, commercial, transactional.
- **Impression:** how many times your URL appeared in search results.
- **CTR:** clicks divided by impressions.
- **Average position:** the average of your URL's highest ranking per appearance. It is an average, not a fixed position.
- **Backlink:** a link from another site to yours.
- **Anchor text:** the clickable text of a link.
- **Canonical:** the URL you designate as the main version among several similar pages.
- **Indexed:** the page is stored in Google's index.
- **Crawl budget:** the number of URLs Google is willing and able to crawl on your site. Rarely a problem for small sites.
- **E-E-A-T:** Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust. A concept from the quality rater guidelines, not a single ranking factor number.
- **Entity:** something recognized as an object (person, place, brand, concept), not just a string of words.
- **Core Web Vitals:** page experience metrics (LCP, INP, CLS).

## Two kinds of metrics you should not mix
- **Google data (GSC):** impressions, clicks, CTR, position. Real data from Google search for your site.
- **Third-party estimates (Ahrefs, Semrush, etc.):** volume, difficulty, DR. These are their model's approximations. Useful as a compass, not as fact.
