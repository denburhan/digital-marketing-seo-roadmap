# 03. Google Discover and Image Optimization

**Level:** Expert | **Reading time:** 15 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 03-advanced

## Goal
Understand the basics of appearing in Discover and prepare images that follow the guidelines.

## Discover
Discover shows content without an active search, based on user interests. It is volatile and cannot be promised. Principles from Google guidance and related announcements:
- Descriptive titles, no clickbait, no hiding the content (curiosity gap).
- Content that prioritizes local relevance for the user's country.
- Authority per topic.
- Large, high-quality images.

## Images
- Use images at least 1200 pixels wide and enable `max-image-preview:large` in the robots meta to allow large image display, according to Google's Discover documentation.
- Descriptive file names, meaningful alt text, captions when helpful.
- Modern formats (WebP, AVIF) and compression.
- Do not create misleading images or images with deceptive text.
- EXIF and geotags are not a ranking factor Google has ever stated.

## Original images are more valuable
Photos of real work support first-hand experience (the E-E-A-T module). If you generate images with AI, make sure they do not mislead about reality (for example, work results that never existed).

## Measure
The Discover report in GSC (if the site qualifies) and the Search Appearance report for images.

## Exercise
Audit 10 articles: main image size, alt text, title, and max-image-preview setting.
