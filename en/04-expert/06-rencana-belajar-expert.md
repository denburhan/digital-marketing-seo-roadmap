# 06. Expert Study Plan (12 weeks)

**Prerequisite:** pass the 03-advanced criteria

- **Week 1-3:** AI search and GEO. Result: a monthly manual test protocol.
- **Week 4-6:** topical authority. Result: a topic map and a 90-day plan.
- **Week 7-8:** Discover and images. Result: audit and fixes.
- **Week 9-10:** strategy and decisions. Result: 2 decision reports.
- **Week 11-12:** automation. Result: a weekly data pipeline.

## Final portfolio
One real case study with before and after data from GSC. Write what was measured, what was not, and what you would do differently. Do not polish the numbers. An honest case study is worth more than a perfect one that cannot be proven.
