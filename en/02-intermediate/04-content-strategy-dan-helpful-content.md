# 04. Content Strategy and Helpful Content

**Level:** Intermediate | **Reading time:** 18 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 02-intermediate/01

## Goal
Plan content that helps readers and is hard to copy, not just content that fills a keyword.

## Questions Google asks about helpful content
Google's guidance on content made for people stresses things like: does the content give original information or analysis, does the reader feel they learned enough after reading, and is there evidence of first-hand experience. Read the original guidance on Google Search Central and use it as your rubric.

## Commodity content vs valuable content
A simple test (heuristic): if a machine can write a similar version in seconds without any data or experience, the content is a commodity. Hard-to-copy differentiators: original photos and data, real test results, interviews, documented work processes.

## Fabricated experience is a debt
Do not write "our client grew by X percent" or "we have handled X projects" if you cannot prove it. One false claim damages trust in the whole site. It is better to write what you truly did, however small, complete with evidence.

## Content calendar
1. Start from the BOFU and MOFU clusters closest to the business.
2. Add TOFU that answers real customer questions.
3. Schedule updates of old content, not only new production.
4. Each article has: target query, intent, funnel stage, evidence to include, internal link destinations.

## Content updates
Update when the information changes or the data is outdated. Changing the date without changing the content is not an update.

## Structure readers like
- Core answer at the start.
- Short paragraphs, one idea per paragraph.
- Descriptive headings.
- Real examples, tables, or images when they help.

## Exercise
1. Pick one of your old articles. Add one piece of original evidence (photo, data, real steps).
2. Write an 8-article calendar with the columns listed above.

## Comprehension check
- What is the long-term consequence of an experience claim you cannot prove?
- What is the difference between updating content and just changing the date?
