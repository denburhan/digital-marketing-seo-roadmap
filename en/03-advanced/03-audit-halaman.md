# 03. Page Audit

**Level:** Advanced | **Reading time:** 20 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 02-intermediate

## Goal
Produce a single-page audit that ends in a prioritized action list, not just a list of findings.

## Five-layer framework
1. **Intent.** What are people looking for with the target query? What page type wins in the SERP?
2. **Content.** Does it answer the intent better than competitors? Is there original evidence? Are there gaps you can fill?
3. **Structure.** Headings, answer order, internal links, visual elements.
4. **Technical.** Index, canonical, speed, mobile, structured data.
5. **Trust and conversion.** Identity, proof, a clear call to action.

## How to assess
Give each layer a status: pass, needs improvement, fail. Add a one-sentence reason. Avoid numeric scores that look scientific but have no measuring tool behind them.

## Prioritize
Order actions with two questions:
1. How large is the reasonable impact on the goal (clicks, leads, sales)?
2. How easy is it to do?

Do high-impact, easy ones first. End with one firm recommendation backed by numbers from GSC or analytics.

## Template
Use `templates/audit-halaman.md`.

## Exercise
Audit one page ranking 5 to 15 for a valuable query. Produce at most 5 prioritized actions and one main recommendation.

## Comprehension check
- Why should a tool's "SEO 87/100" score not be the sole basis for a decision?
- How do you decide action priority?
