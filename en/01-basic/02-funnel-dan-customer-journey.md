# 02. Funnel and Customer Journey

**Level:** Basic | **Reading time:** 12 minutes | **Prerequisite:** module 01

## Goal
Understand the journey of a potential buyer and map content types to each stage.

## The three stages used in this repo
- **TOFU (Top of Funnel):** people just realized they have a problem. They look for information. Example: "how to choose parquet flooring".
- **MOFU (Middle of Funnel):** people compare options. Example: "parquet vs vinyl", "alternative to X".
- **BOFU (Bottom of Funnel):** people are ready to act. Example: "parquet installation service Surabaya", "parquet price per meter".

Other models (AIDA, See-Think-Do-Care, the AARRR pirate funnel) basically split the same thing under different names. Pick one and use it consistently.

## Intent modifier rules
Words inside a query often force the funnel stage:
- Educational or how-to words (DIY, guide, what is) tend to be TOFU.
- Comparison, review, and alternative tend to be MOFU.
- Commercial or transactional words (price, service, buy, order, near me) tend to be BOFU.

This is a heuristic, not a law. If in doubt, look at which pages actually appear in the search results for that query.

## TOFU and MOFU feed BOFU
Educational articles rarely close a sale by themselves. Their job is to bring people in, build trust, then guide them to a service or product page through reasonable internal links. Do not force every article to become a sales page.

## Content mapping
| Stage | Content format | Page goal | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | guide articles, video, infographics | understood and read | impressions, clicks, reading time |
| MOFU | comparisons, case studies, checklists | considered | clicks to service page, email sign-ups |
| BOFU | service, product, and pricing pages | acted on | orders, form submissions, chats |

## Exercise
1. Write 3 TOFU, 3 MOFU, and 3 BOFU queries for your chosen business.
2. Mark which query is closest to money, and which is easiest to win.

## Comprehension check
- Why should not all content lead directly to a sale?
- What do you do when the stage of a query is ambiguous?
