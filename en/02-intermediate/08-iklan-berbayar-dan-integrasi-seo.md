# 08. Paid Ads and SEO Integration

**Level:** Intermediate | **Reading time:** 15 minutes | **Prerequisite:** 01-basic/01

## Goal
Understand the basics of paid advertising and how it complements SEO.

## Common platforms
- **Google Ads (Search):** ads based on queries. Suited to BOFU intent.
- **Meta Ads and TikTok Ads:** ads based on audience and interest. Suited to building awareness and retargeting.
- **Marketplace ads:** ads inside buying and selling platforms.

## Basic concepts
- **CPC:** cost per click. **CPM:** cost per thousand impressions. **CPA:** cost per action. **ROAS:** ad revenue divided by ad cost.
- Determine the margin per sale first. A ROAS that looks good can still lose money if the margin is thin.

## Operational safety
Do not change a running campaign without the account owner's approval. Test changes in a separate campaign or with a small budget, and record what changed and when.

## Synergy with SEO
- Test messages and titles in Search ads to find the most clicked angle, then use that insight in organic titles.
- Use ad query data to find keywords you had not thought of.
- Retarget readers of TOFU articles with MOFU or BOFU offers.

## Landing pages
Ads that point to a slow or irrelevant page waste money. Align the ad message with the page content.

## Exercise
1. Calculate the ROAS break-even point for one product from its gross margin (use real data, or just write the formula).
2. Write 3 ad headline variants and a hypothesis for each.

## Comprehension check
- Why does a high ROAS not always mean profit?
- How can ad data help SEO research?
